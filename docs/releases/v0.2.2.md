# SnapMark 0.2.2 — Movable video clips and edited playback

- Drag split video clips left/right to reorder them, with selection and an
  insertion marker. Undo/redo and saved projects preserve clip order.
- Delete a selected clip to close its gap and shorten the sequence immediately.
  Preview, scrubbing, seek buttons, frame stepping, timestamps and export all
  follow the edited sequence. Removed footage is excluded from the preview,
  including buffered frames and paused-frame display.
- Keep annotations attached to source footage when clips move or are deleted;
  project them into the assembled timeline.
- Add Ctrl+wheel zoom centered on the cursor, horizontal scrollbar/trackpad and
  Shift+wheel panning, an adaptive ruler, playhead following and Fit-to-sequence.
- Verify reordered video and audio with real FFmpeg exports, plus real Qt preview
  playback of the edited clip order.

The source recording is preserved. The package targets Ubuntu 25.10 amd64.
Screen-capture platform verification limits remain documented in docs/video.md.
